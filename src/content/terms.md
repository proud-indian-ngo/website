## Volunteering

Volunteers must be 18 or older. Volunteering with Proud Indian is unpaid and voluntary. It does not make you our employee, agent or partner, and it gives you no right to pay, benefits or future work. If we pay back costs you had to meet for a programme, that is a reimbursement under our usual approval process, not payment for your time.

When you volunteer with us, you agree to:

- follow our [safeguarding rules](/volunteer/#safeguarding) and our child-safeguarding policy whenever you are with children in our programmes;
- follow the instructions of your team lead and the people running the session;
- treat children, families, partners and other volunteers with respect;
- keep private what you learn about the children and families we work with, and not share their photos or details yourself.

We may decline a request to volunteer, or end someone's participation, at our discretion, especially where the safety of children is concerned. We will usually explain why, but we don't have to when doing so could put someone at risk.

## Dashboard accounts

To volunteer, you need an account on the dashboard. When you have one:

- give us accurate details and keep them up to date;
- keep your password safe and don't share your account. You are responsible for what happens under it;
- have only one account, for yourself. Don't create an account for someone else, or more than one for yourself;
- tell us at connect@proudindian.ngo if you think someone else has used your account.

We may suspend or close an account that is used to break these terms, to harm others, or to misuse the dashboard or the data in it. Where we can, we will tell you first. You can ask us to delete your account at any time, as the privacy policy explains.

## Using Proud Indian in an AI assistant

You can use Proud Indian in ChatGPT, Claude and other assistants that support it. [Proud Indian in ChatGPT and Claude](/assistants/) explains what it can do and how to add it.

- **The assistant is not ours.** ChatGPT is run by OpenAI and Claude by Anthropic, under their own terms and privacy policies. We are responsible only for our connector and our dashboard, not for the assistant or what it says.
- **Check before you confirm.** AI assistants can make mistakes. They can get a date, a place or a detail wrong, or misunderstand what you asked. Check the session and the details the assistant is about to send before you confirm, and rely on the dashboard and this website for the final word.
- **It counts as yours once you confirm.** A sign-up made through an assistant counts as yours only once you confirm your email address. A session request made through an assistant counts as yours only if you have connected your own account. Don't sign up anyone else, or use details that aren't yours.
- **You can disconnect at any time.** Disconnect Proud Indian in the assistant's settings, or write to us and we will remove the connection.

Session requests made through an assistant follow the same rules as those made in the dashboard: your team lead approves them, and these terms apply to you as a volunteer.

## Donations

Donations are made through our Razorpay payment page and processed by Razorpay and its payment partners under their own terms. We never see your card, UPI or bank details.

All donations are voluntary. Proud Indian is registered under section 80G of the Income Tax Act, 1961. If you add your PAN when you donate, we email you an 80G receipt, and Form 10BE after the financial year ends. Please make sure the details you give are correct, since we report them to the Income Tax Department.

If you have a question about a donation, or think a payment was made in error, write to us at connect@proudindian.ngo with your Razorpay payment ID, and we will look into it.
